About Me

Most of what I know about software did not come from a lecture hall. It came from getting stuck on a problem, staying with it, understanding why it failed, and refusing to stop until it worked. That persistence, combined with a Computer Science background, has shaped the way I approach engineering.

I build complete web applications across the stack, from responsive React interfaces and carefully structured HTML/CSS to backend services, APIs, authentication, databases, dashboards, and deployment. I enjoy understanding the entire journey of a system: how an interaction begins in the browser, travels through application logic, reaches a database, and ultimately produces something useful for the person using it.

My experience also extends beyond software development. Working in administration and operations taught me the value of accurate documentation, clear communication, structured processes, dependable follow-through, and understanding how organizations actually operate. Those lessons now influence the systems I build and the way I work with people.

Today, my work sits at the intersection of technology, business, and practical problem-solving. Through Senzy Enterprises, I am building digital products and exploring larger systems that turn complex organizational needs into useful technology.

I am particularly interested in software that does more than look good. I want it to make work clearer, information easier to manage, decisions easier to make, and organizations more capable.
